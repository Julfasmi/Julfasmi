## 🧠 How I Approach Data Problems

I believe a good data solution is not simply the model with the highest accuracy or the dashboard with the most charts.

My typical approach is:

```text
Understand the Problem
        ↓
Collect & Validate Data
        ↓
Clean & Prepare Data
        ↓
Explore & Analyze
        ↓
Build Models / Solutions
        ↓
Evaluate Results
        ↓
Visualize & Communicate
        ↓
Deploy / Automate
        ↓
Deliver Actionable Insights
```

I try to balance **technical correctness, interpretability, maintainability, and practical business value** when developing a solution.
